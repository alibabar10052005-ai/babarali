How does our Estimating Process work?

We methodically review the client’s drawing plans and specifications to formulate a precise masonry estimate according to the plan, elevation, and section views to define the scope of work. Under our masonry takeoff outsourcing services, we measure the quantities by point and click method using masonry takeoff software like Planswift, Bluebeam, etc. After which, the quantities are imported into EXCEL spreadsheets organized under the MasterFormat. We also follow CSI codes for mentioned items in the drawings and plans.
